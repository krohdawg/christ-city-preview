OUR STORY

WHO WE ARE

Kenny and Kim have been going to church together since middle school. That’s also when both of them first sensed a call to ministry, and they have been following it for 26 years.

For most of that time the work was split. Kim worked in teaching and social work while Kenny served in pastoral ministry. Then they both felt a shift toward doing it together.

Christ City is the church they are planting side by side, and they will lead it as co-pastors.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

We aren’t here to fight culture wars or win arguments. We want to look at Jesus and live the way he lived, so that when you go home, what you remember is Jesus.

OUR VISION

To see our city renewed by Christ through people who live like Jesus.

HOUSE CHURCHES

WHAT IS A HOUSE CHURCH?

Christ City is being built as a church of house churches, in homes across La Mirada, Norwalk, Whittier, Bellflower and wherever our people live.

  1. 1. IN A HOME

    A house church is a group of neighbors who meet in a living room close to where they live.

  2. 2. A REAL CHURCH

    It is more than a small group. Each house will have its own pastors and leaders, care for its own neighborhood and grow by starting new houses.

  3. 3. TOGETHER ON SUNDAY

    Then everyone gathers on Sunday to worship as one church, and goes back out into the city.
